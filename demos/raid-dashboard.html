Summary

How are the red, amber and green decided?

Only open items count (status is not closed). Colours always come with a word and a shape.

  • Open risks and issues: red if any is critical, amber if any is high, otherwise green.
  • Overdue: red if any is critical or there are 4 or more, amber for 1 to 3, green for none.
  • High or critical: red for 6 or more, amber for 1 to 5, green for none. Counts all four types.
  • Due this fortnight: items due from the as-of date to 13 days later. Red if 3 or more are high or critical, amber if any are due, green for none.
