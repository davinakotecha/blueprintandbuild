IBM Thinkle

How to play

Guess the AI, consulting or IBM term in 6 tries. Terms are 4–7 letters long, and the board shows today's length.

Each guess must be a real word. After each guess the tiles show how close you were.

IDEAS

I is in the term and in the right spot.

TEAMS

M is in the term but in the wrong spot.

PITCH

C is not in the term.

A new Thinkle every weekday

New puzzles go live at midnight, Monday to Friday. At the weekend you still get Friday's. Bank holidays and Christmas week never break your streak.

  • Hint shows the category and a clue. Using it adds 💡 to your share.
  • Hard mode makes you reuse every letter you've found. It's marked * on your share.
  • Past puzzles lets you catch up on days you missed. They don't count towards your stats.

Your progress stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. To move your stats, use Settings → Back up.
